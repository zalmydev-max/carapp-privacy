Delete your Car App data

Car App by YidAssist

Car App has no accounts. Everything it saves is on your phone only, so you delete it yourself, right away.

In the app

  1. Open Car App and go to Me.
  2. Tap Delete my data, then Delete my data again to confirm.

This erases your cars, tickets, statements, wallet, photos, rentals, trips and costs from your phone and turns off the app's reminders. If you turned on the weekly plate check or Auto-detect parking, they stop too.

Or uninstall

Uninstalling Car App removes everything it saved on your phone.

What we keep

Nothing. We have no server copy of your data and no account to delete. Crash reports (technical only, no personal data) are deleted by Firebase Crashlytics after 90 days.
