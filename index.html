Privacy policy

Car App by YidAssist · Last updated November 5, 2026 (Car App 1.0.13)

Short version. Car App has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your car data, statements, photos and papers stay on your phone. No ads, no selling data, no tracking. A few features send one small piece of information (like a ticket number, a plate or your location) to a public service to get an answer. Each one is listed below.

No account

You do not sign up or log in. We never ask for your email or phone number. A name you type stays on your phone.

What stays on your phone

  • Cars and car data: plate, VIN, tickets, notes, trips, costs, bills, lease and insurance details, service log, reminders and saved parking spots.
  • Bank and insurance statements: when you add a statement (PDF, photo or file), it is read on your phone only. The statement and the amounts found in it are never uploaded.
  • Photos and scans: tickets, meter receipts, your parking spot, dashboard lights, and your license, registration and insurance card in the Wallet. Wallet scans are encrypted on your phone and can be locked with your fingerprint, face or a PIN.
  • Rental mode: the rental agreement or plate photo you snap, the pick-up and return photos of the car and the gas gauge, and the rental's dates and charges. Read and kept on your phone only.
  • Bluetooth car connection (Auto-detect parking, off unless you turn it on): the app asks for the Nearby devices (Bluetooth) permission only when you turn the feature on. It remembers which paired car you picked and when it connects or disconnects, so it can ask "Did you park?". This stays on your phone and is never sent anywhere.

The app's data is not included in Android's cloud backup.

What the app sends, and to whom

Only when you use the feature. Nothing is linked to you, because the app has no account or ID for you. All connections are encrypted (HTTPS).

What is sentToWhy
Ticket number, or your plate and stateNYC Open Data (City of New York)To show a ticket's amount and status. If you turn on Check my plate weekly, your plate and state are sent once a week to find new tickets. Turn it off any time in Me.
Your location or the street of your parked carNYC Open Data (parking signs, meters)To find street-cleaning and meter rules where you parked.
VIN, or year, make and modelNHTSA (U.S. government) and fueleconomy.gov (U.S. DOE)To decode your car, show recalls and safety ratings, and fill in its MPG.
Your area (state or region)U.S. EIATo show the average gas price where you are.
Your current locationU.S. DOE (AFDC) and OpenStreetMap (Overpass)To find gas stations and EV chargers near you.
Your location or an address you typeOpenStreetMap (Nominatim, Photon), NYC GeoSearch, OSRMTo turn a position into an address and to plan a trip route.
Your approximate locationOpen-MeteoFor winter and weather tips.
The map area around your spotOpenFreeMap, EsriTo draw maps.

Location is used only while you use a feature that needs it, and only if you allow it. It is never collected in the background.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, it sends a crash report to Firebase Crashlytics (Google) so we can fix the bug. A report has technical details only: the app version, phone model, Android version and where in the code the crash happened, with a random install ID. It contains no names, plates, statements, photos or location. Firebase Analytics and remote push messages are turned off; the app makes no advertising ID.

What we do not do

  • No ads and no advertising ID.
  • We do not sell or share your data.
  • No analytics or tracking across apps or websites.
  • No background location.

Notifications

Reminders (meter, alternate side parking, ticket and bill due dates, rental return) are scheduled on your phone. They are not sent from a server.

Links you open

Official pages (NYC DOT, NYC Department of Finance, NYS DMV, NHTSA and others), maps and stores open in your browser or their app and follow their own privacy policies. Car App is not affiliated with any government agency.

Children

The app is meant for drivers and is not directed at children.

Deleting your data

In the app: Me › Delete my data erases everything the app saved on your phone and turns off its reminders. Uninstalling the app also removes it. We hold no copy anywhere else. More: how to delete your data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date above and tell you in the app for important changes.
